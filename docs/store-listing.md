# Play Store listing copy

Paste into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing.
Play indexes the app name, short description and full description for search, so
the phrases people actually type — "AI fitness coach", "workout plan", "calorie
counter", "macro tracker", "home workout" — appear naturally throughout.

## App name (30 characters max)

```
Celia: AI Fitness Coach
```

Alternative, if you want the nutrition side in the title:

```
Celia: AI Workout & Calories
```

## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Your AI fitness coach: custom workouts, calorie counter and macro tracking.
```

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Celia is your AI fitness coach: a workout plan built around you, a calorie counter that reads your plate, and a coach you can actually talk to — in one app.

BUILD A WORKOUT PLAN IN SECONDS
Tell Celia what you want — 20 minutes at home with no equipment, an hour at the gym with dumbbells, abs and core, upper body, stretching and mobility — and she builds a complete workout routine around your time, your equipment and your goal. Every exercise comes with a looping demonstration, so you always know the movement.

TRAIN WITH GUIDED ROUTINES
Press play and follow along. The workout player moves through each exercise on a timer with short form cues, so you are never counting reps or watching the clock. Save the routines that work, favourite the ones you love, and repeat them any day.

AN AI PERSONAL TRAINER YOU CAN TALK TO
Ask for a training plan, a healthy meal plan for weight loss or muscle gain, or dinner ideas from the ingredients already in your kitchen. Celia knows your routines, your meals and your progress, so the answer fits your plan and not somebody else's.

CALORIE COUNTER AND FOOD SCANNER
Point your camera at your meal and Celia estimates the calories, protein, carbs and fat. Log it in a tap and correct anything that looks off. No barcode hunting, no scrolling through a database for the right entry.

CALORIE AND MACRO TARGETS THAT FIT YOU
Enter your weight, height, age and gender and Celia works out your daily calorie budget and macro targets. Your home screen shows where you stand at a glance: calories left, protein, carbs and fat.

STAY CONSISTENT
A day streak counts every day you train or log a meal, and your level climbs as you complete workouts. Small, visible progress is what keeps you coming back.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• AI workout plan generator: home or gym, any equipment, any time budget
• Guided workout player with exercise demonstrations and form cues
• Hundreds of exercises: strength, calisthenics, HIIT, stretching and mobility
• AI calorie counter from a photo
• Meal log with calories, protein, carbs and fat
• Personalised daily calorie and macro targets
• Day streaks and levels to keep you on track
• AI coach chat for workouts, nutrition and meal planning
• Light and dark mode
• No ads

FOR EVERY GOAL
Weight loss, fat loss, muscle gain, strength, general fitness, flexibility and mobility — whether you train at home with no equipment, with a pair of dumbbells, or at a full gym.

Celia is not a medical service. Speak to a doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition programme.

Questions or feedback: contact@the-fit.eu
```

## Screenshot guidance

Play shows the first two or three screenshots in search results, so lead with
the strongest. Requirements: 2 to 8 phone screenshots, PNG or JPEG, each between
320 px and 3840 px on its shortest and longest side.

Suggested order, one message per shot:

1. Home screen with Today's progress — "Your day at a glance"
2. Routine player mid-exercise — "Guided workouts with demos"
3. Chat asking Celia for a routine — "Ask for any workout"
4. Calorie scanner result — "Scan a meal, get the calories"
5. Nutrition screen with targets — "Calories and macros that fit you"
6. Profile with streak and level — "Build a streak"
